# India map attribution

`india-map.svg` is the unmodified "Map of India" from the
[`@svg-maps/india`](https://github.com/VictorCazanave/svg-maps/tree/master/packages/india)
package (v2.0.0) by Victor Cazanave, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

It is drawn in a Mercator projection. Journey Logs places pins on it with
`x = 20.9295·lon − 1427.061` and `y = 837.713 − 20.9608·mercator(lat)`
(mercator in degrees), a fit against known landmarks (Chandigarh, Delhi,
Goa, Sikkim, Tripura, Dadra & Nagar Haveli) with 0.3px RMS error on the
612×696 viewBox; see CLAUDE.md "Journey Logs module".
